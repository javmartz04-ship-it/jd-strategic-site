About Josh

Raised in franchising. Now on your side of the table.

I grew up in franchising. My family went from independent restaurant owners, to multi-unit franchisees, to building a dry-cleaning brand from a handful of locations to more than 2,400. I have lived every side of it, and now I am opening my own franchise while I help you open yours.

The insiderJosh DuBoisFounder, JD Strategic. Second-generation franchise family. FranServe certified.

The story

Second generation. Both sides of the deal.

The restaurants

My family started as independent restaurant owners in hospitality. No system, no playbook, no brand behind us. Mornings, nights, and weekends, and no way to build the systems to escape the daily operations.

The turn

Skeptical of franchising for years, then a second conversation with a dry-cleaning brand changed it. My family became franchisees and are multi-unit owners today, ten years later, still operating four locations in Massachusetts from Florida.

The franchisor side

We got the chance to lead the brand when it was stuck at ten to fifteen locations, invested in the franchise systems, and scaled it past 2,400 locations across the world.

The lesson that stuck

A strong franchisor has to be an expert in two things at once: the industry, and franchising itself. That is the bar I hold every brand to. A food franchise that worked for us despite a weak franchisor is why I know the good, the bad, and the ugly.

The seed

I held nearly every job in the family business and managed one of the dry-cleaning franchises in high school. I noticed the owner made money without being at the location. Ownership done right is not buying yourself a job.

Corporate finance

Graduated early, took the safe path into finance and asset-management recruiting, and learned how to evaluate businesses and people. I also saw people earning well over seven figures who still dreaded Monday. There is no safe path.

The bet

Went to the International Franchise Expo to buy a franchise and found franchise consulting instead. Took a six-figure loan, walked away from a remote six-figure salary, and started JD Strategic on nothing but belief in my own work ethic.

Today

A FranServe-certified franchise coach, working with about ten people a month as a free resource. And I am opening my own franchise in the Miami market, the first of a portfolio, walking through the same door I coach you through.

I've lived every side of it, the good deals and the bad ones.

What I am building toward

Everything I do is built around three pillars.

01

Financial freedom

Equity in something you own, not a salary with an expiration date your boss never tells you about. Every job has one.

02

Lifestyle freedom

Being the CEO of the business, not the one stuck in it. My family runs four locations in Massachusetts from Florida. That is the model working.

03

A lasting legacy

Something to build on and something to pass down. Every family I help navigate this the right way now has both.

Business ownership puts you in the driver’s seat of your life instead of the passenger seat. You already have the skill set. The only question is whether you are ready to bet on yourself.
