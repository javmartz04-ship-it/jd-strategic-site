How it works

Five steps. Zero guesswork.

Most people waste months in biased online rankings and pay-to-play lists and end up no closer to a decision. I flip that. It starts with you and what matters to you, whether ownership is even right for you, and only then the brands that fit both your goals and my vetting criteria.

Start where you are

Which of these sounds like you?

01

Locked on one brand

You inquired on a brand, it is the top of its space, and it lines up with what you are after. I am not here to get in the way. I make the introduction, share the questions and tips for every step, and you still get the rebate.

02

Looking at a few

You have looked at a few brands across a couple of industries. I confirm they are even available in your market so you do not waste time, run them against my vetting criteria, and flag any others with stronger economics or better support.

03

Open to ownership

You want to own a business and do not yet know what that looks like: which industry, which model, what fits your skills. I do the research and filter thousands of brands down to the vetted few in your market.

What we do with every client

A structured path from curious to confident owner.

Every step is a working session, not a sales call. Typically one to three months, at your pace, with one point of contact the whole way.

Book a Call
01

Introductory call

A short call to get the big picture. Where do you see yourself in three years, what is pulling you toward franchising, and what are you really after. By the end we know whether to move forward now, hold off, or that ownership is not the path. Any of those is a fine answer.

02

Assessment and matching

A brief assessment so I understand how you are wired: strengths, lifestyle, risk tolerance, market. I do the research, check territory availability, and present a tailored shortlist of vetted brands. Together we pick one to three to enter formal conversations with.

03

Discovery calls with the franchisors

You meet the brands directly to understand how the business really operates, what your day looks like as the owner, and the training, support, and marketing behind it. I coach you through every call and send preparation materials before each one.

04

Economics and franchisee validation

The Franchise Disclosure Document, especially Item 19: the financial structure, the fees, your territory rights, the unit economics. Then direct calls with existing owners for the unfiltered version. Many candidates bring in a franchise attorney here, and I guide what to ask.

05

Funding, Discovery Day, and your decision

Funding partners are introduced in the background so you can compare SBA loans, ROBS, and lines of credit without being rushed. Then Discovery Day with the leadership and support teams. Some people move forward. Some do not. Either way, you leave with clarity.
