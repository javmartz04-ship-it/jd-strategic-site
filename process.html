How it works

Five steps. Zero guesswork.

A structured path from curious to confident owner, typically one to three months, at your pace.

Every step is a working session, not a sales call. The document that decides your decade gets read line by line, together.

Book a Call
01

Initial call

A conversation, not a pitch. We cover your goals, capital, and timeline, and I tell you honestly whether franchising fits. If it does not, we say so here.

02

Franchise assessment

A structured questionnaire turns your profile into a shortlist of three to five vetted brands, matched to you rather than to whoever markets hardest.

03

Brand conversations

I set up meetings with each franchisor and coach you through them: what to ask, what the answers actually mean, and what to watch for.

04

Due diligence and validation

Guided FDD review, item by item, plus structured calls with existing franchisees so you hear the unfiltered version before you commit.

05

A confident decision

You choose with clear eyes, or we go back and explore further. No pressure, no deadline. The right answer is the one that holds up in year five.
